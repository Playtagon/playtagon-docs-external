Self-Promotion is the Wizard's ability to offer help when a platform event suggests it is useful.

## MVP Triggers

- Archive upload failed or needs attention.
- Promo assets are incomplete.
- Publishing module is incomplete.
- Review comment needs action.

Prompts should be helpful and contextual, not noisy.
