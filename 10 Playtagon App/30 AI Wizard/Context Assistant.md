Context Assistant is the Wizard behavior inside a specific screen.

## MVP Examples

- In Resources, explain archive build feedback.
- In Launch Game, help interpret a test setup.
- In Promo Assets, identify missing required assets.
- In Publishing, explain incomplete fields and review comments.

Context should improve guidance without changing the underlying workflow rules.
