Wizard Onboarding is the conversational shell around early access flows.

## MVP Paths

- Waitlist request through [[Auth Waitlist]].
- Email invitation through [[Auth Invite Email]].
- Invite link entry through [[Auth Invite Link]].
- Studio selection through [[Auth Multi Studio]].

The goal is to make the first steps clear while preserving the same access rules as Auth.
