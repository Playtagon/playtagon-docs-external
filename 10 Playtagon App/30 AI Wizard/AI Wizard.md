AI Wizard is the assistant layer in Playtagon. It is not a creator type and not a standalone tool. In MVP, it helps users complete the Play SDK path to publishing.

## MVP Focus

- Onboarding and entry flows.
- SDK questions.
- Archive, launch and publishing guidance.
- Promo asset assistance.
- Contextual help inside a game or module.

The Wizard can mention future capabilities only as future direction, not as available product surface.
