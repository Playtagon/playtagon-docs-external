Replays help the team reproduce a specific round or scenario in Launch Game.

## MVP Use Cases

- Verify an issue reported during testing.
- Share a reproducible state with a developer or reviewer.
- Confirm behavior after a new archive upload.

Replay support depends on the data available from the launch runtime and the game archive.
