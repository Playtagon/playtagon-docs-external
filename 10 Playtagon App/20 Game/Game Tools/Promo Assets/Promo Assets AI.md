Promo Assets AI helps the team create or refine promotional artwork descriptions and variants.

## MVP Help

- Generate asset concepts from a game description.
- Adapt artwork to required sizes.
- Suggest missing asset types.
- Help prepare assets for approval.

Final approval remains part of the Promo Assets workflow.
