Resources is where the team uploads the `.playtagon` archive and supporting files for a game.

## MVP Responsibilities

- Store game files used by the studio.
- Upload a versioned `.playtagon` archive.
- Send the archive for build and validation.
- Show build status and errors.
- Make a successful archive version available to [[Launch Game]] and [[Publishing Math]].

Resources is the bridge between the developer build and the Playtagon review workflow.
