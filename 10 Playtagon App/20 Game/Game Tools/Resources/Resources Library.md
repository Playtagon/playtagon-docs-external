Resources Library stores supporting material that belongs to the game workspace.

## MVP Scope

The library can hold files the team needs while preparing the game, but the publishing-critical artifact remains the versioned `.playtagon` archive. Promo artwork that must be approved for publishing belongs in [[Promo Assets]].
