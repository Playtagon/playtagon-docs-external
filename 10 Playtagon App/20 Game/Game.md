A Game is the workspace for one Playtagon game. In MVP, it connects the Play SDK archive, launch testing, promo assets and publishing submission.

## MVP Tabs

- Hub: status widgets and shortcuts to game tools.
- Insights: game-level performance and readiness signals.
- Settings: game identity and team access.

## MVP Tools

- [[Resources]] for `.playtagon` archive upload and files.
- [[Launch Game]] for archive-based testing.
- [[Promo Assets]] for required artwork.
- [[Publishing]] for review and live deployment.

The game is created through [[Types Play SDK]] only.
