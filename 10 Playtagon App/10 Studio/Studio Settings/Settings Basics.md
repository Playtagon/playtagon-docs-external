Basic Settings stores the studio identity shown in Playtagon.

## MVP Fields

- Studio name.
- Studio logo.
- Studio slug.
- Brand information when required for publishing context.

Brand approval and operator-side setup may involve internal operations that are not exposed in this external documentation.
