AI Wizard Settings define how the assistant behaves for the studio.

## MVP Scope

- Assistant naming and tone preferences.
- Studio-level instructions that help the Wizard answer in context.
- Boundaries for SDK support and publishing guidance.

The Wizard should support the MVP workflow without presenting future tools as available features.
