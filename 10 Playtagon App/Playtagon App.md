Playtagon App is the web workspace where a studio creates a game through the Play SDK path, uploads a `.playtagon` archive, tests it in Launch Game, prepares promo assets and submits the game for publishing.

## MVP Scope

The MVP keeps the product focused on the path from studio access to a live game:

1. Sign in or enter through an invite.
2. Create or select a studio.
3. Create a game with the Play SDK creator type.
4. Upload a `.playtagon` archive in [[Resources]].
5. Test the archive in [[Launch Game]].
6. Prepare required artwork in [[Promo Assets]].
7. Complete [[Publishing]] and move an approved submission toward live.
